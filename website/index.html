What We Do

End-to-End Energy Audit Services

From walk-through assessments to detailed investment-grade audits, we cover the full spectrum of energy accounting — electricity, fuels, steam, heat, compressed air, and water.

Walk-Through Audit

A preliminary survey to identify obvious energy losses, low-hanging savings, and priority areas for detailed investigation.

Detailed Energy Audit

On-site data collection, metering, and analysis of significant energy users (SEUs) with technical and financial savings evaluation.

DOE Portal Submission

Step-by-step guidance for first-time and recurring submissions to the DOE Energy Efficiency and Conservation portal.

RA 11285 Compliance

Documentation, reporting, and roadmap development aligned with the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act.

Energy Management System

Help establishing an ISO 50001-aligned energy management framework and continuous improvement program.

Measurement & Verification

Post-implementation M&V to validate savings, baseline corrections, and IPMVP-aligned reporting.
