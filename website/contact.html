FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does an energy audit take?

A walk-through audit typically takes 1–3 days on-site, with a report delivered within 2 weeks. A detailed investment-grade audit ranges from 3–8 weeks end-to-end depending on facility complexity.

Are we required by law to conduct an energy audit?

If your annual energy consumption qualifies you as a Designated Establishment under RA 11285, yes — periodic audits and annual reports to the DOE are mandatory.

How much does an audit cost?

Cost depends on facility size, complexity, and audit depth. We provide fixed-fee proposals after a free 30-minute consultation. Most audits pay for themselves within the first year of implementation.

Do you work outside Metro Manila?

Yes. We handle engagements nationwide — from Luzon to Visayas to Mindanao.

Can you help with implementation after the audit?

Absolutely. We support tender preparation, vendor evaluation, and Measurement & Verification (M&V) after measures are implemented — though we remain independent and do not sell equipment.
