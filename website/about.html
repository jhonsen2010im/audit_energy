Why energy audits matter in the Philippines

Beyond compliance, audits unlock real savings — typically 10–30% of annual energy spend for facilities that have never undertaken one.

RA 11285 in brief

The Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act institutionalizes energy efficiency as a national way of life. Designated Establishments are required to appoint an Energy Conservation Officer, file annual reports, and undergo periodic audits.

Who is a Designated Establishment?

Type 1 (Industrial & Commercial): annual energy consumption of 500,000 to 4,000,000 kWh-equivalent. Type 2: above 4,000,000 kWh-equivalent. Thresholds may also apply to transport and government facilities.

What you need to file

Annual Energy Consumption Report (AECR), Annual Energy Efficiency and Conservation Report (AEECR), and an Energy Conservation Plan — all submitted via the DOE online portal.

How we help
