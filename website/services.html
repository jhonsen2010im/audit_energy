Our Services

Energy audits engineered for results

From rapid walk-throughs to investment-grade detailed audits, every engagement is structured around your facility’s operational and compliance goals.

01

Walk-Through Energy Audit

A focused on-site survey to identify obvious energy losses, behavioral issues, and quick-win opportunities. Ideal for facilities at the start of their efficiency journey or preparing for a detailed audit.

  • ✓ Visual inspection of major energy systems
  • ✓ Review of 12 months of utility billing data
  • ✓ Top-10 opportunity list with rough savings estimates
  • ✓ Roadmap for next-step actions

Best for

Small to mid-sized facilities, first-time clients, and pre-audit scoping.

Typical duration

1–3 days on-site

02

Detailed (Investment-Grade) Energy Audit

Comprehensive measurement, analysis, and engineering economic evaluation of every Significant Energy Use (SEU). Outputs a bankable report your finance team can act on.

  • ✓ Power, thermal, and compressed-air metering
  • ✓ Load profiles and equipment benchmarking
  • ✓ Energy and mass balances for major systems
  • ✓ Engineering calculations with payback & IRR per measure

Best for

Manufacturing plants, hospitals, hotels, and large commercial properties.

Typical duration

3–8 weeks end-to-end

03

DOE Energy Audit Report & Portal Submission

We prepare and submit your Energy Efficiency and Conservation reports to the DOE online portal — from initial registration to annual filings. We walk you through it step-by-step.

  • ✓ Designated Establishment registration support
  • ✓ Annual Energy Consumption Report (AECR)
  • ✓ Annual Energy Efficiency & Conservation Report (AEECR)
  • ✓ Energy Conservation Plan documentation

Best for

Designated Establishments under RA 11285 with reporting obligations.

Outputs

Fully accepted DOE submission with audit trail

04

RA 11285 Compliance Advisory

End-to-end advisory on the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act — from understanding your obligations to building an internal compliance program that lasts.

  • ✓ Compliance gap assessment
  • ✓ Energy Conservation Officer (ECO) training support
  • ✓ Internal energy policy and KPI development
  • ✓ Continuous improvement framework

Best for

Organizations building their first formal energy program.

Engagement

One-off advisory or retainer

05

Measurement & Verification (M&V)

After implementing efficiency measures, we verify the savings actually delivered. Reports follow IPMVP principles and stand up to third-party scrutiny.

  • ✓ Pre- and post-installation metering
  • ✓ Baseline development & weather/production normalization
  • ✓ IPMVP-aligned M&V plans
  • ✓ Verified savings reports for finance and management

Best for

Performance contracts, ESCO projects, and CSR reporting.

Engagement

Per measure or full-portfolio basis

06

Energy Management System Setup (ISO 50001-aligned)

Establish a structured energy management system that turns one-time audit findings into a culture of continuous improvement.

  • ✓ Energy review and baseline (EnB)
  • ✓ Energy Performance Indicators (EnPIs)
  • ✓ Internal procedures and documentation
  • ✓ Management review readiness

Best for

Companies pursuing ISO 50001 certification or sustainability reporting.

Engagement

3–6 month implementation

Our Process

How an engagement runs

  1. 1

    Discovery Call

    Free 30-minute consultation to scope your facility and goals.

  2. 2

    Proposal

    Fixed-scope, fixed-fee proposal with deliverables and timeline.

  3. 3

    On-Site Audit

    Data collection, metering, and interviews with operations staff.

  4. 4

    Analysis

    Engineering analysis, SEU identification, and savings modeling.

  5. 5

    Final Report

    Bankable report with prioritized measures and payback.

  6. 6

    Implementation Support

    Optional follow-through on bidding, vendor selection, and M&V.
