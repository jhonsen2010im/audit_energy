Who this is for

Three engagement profiles I take on.

Profile A

Multi-site BPO & shared-services operators with Copilot deployments

You run 800+ seats across two or more Metro Manila floors, and your tenant or your client has started asking about both kWh intensity and AI usage policy in the same RFP.

Trigger events

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot or equivalent rolled out floor-wide in the last 12 months.
  • RA 11285 reporting cycle approaching, or a missed filing in the last cycle.
  • Enterprise client requesting an AI acceptable-use attestation.

Profile B

REIT & landlord portfolios with smart-building BMS+AI layers

You hold a portfolio where a vendor has installed (or is selling) a BMS-overlay AI optimizer, and your tenants are now asking for Scope 2 numbers that tie back to a defensible IPMVP baseline.

Trigger events

  • New BMS or AI-optimization vendor selected in the last 18 months.
  • Tenant ESG questionnaire requiring metered Scope 2 disclosure.
  • REIT board asking for an independent view on vendor savings claims.

Profile C

Type 1 manufacturers piloting predictive-maintenance AI

You are a Designated Establishment under RA 11285 running an AI pilot — vibration analytics, anomaly detection, computer vision QA — on equipment that also dominates your plant load.

Trigger events

  • Predictive-maintenance or vision-QA pilot moving from POC to production.
  • Parent company requesting ISO/IEC 42001 alignment within 12 months.
  • Annual DOE-CECO energy report due, with no IPMVP-grade M&V in place.
